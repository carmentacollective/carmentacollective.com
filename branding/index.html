07 / A little care

A little care

Thanks for helping the mark feel at home.

  • Keep clear space around the mark so it has room to breathe.
  • Use the supplied colors or one of the monochrome versions.
  • Don't recolor, stretch, rotate, or add effects to the vector mark.
  • Keep it off busy backgrounds. Choose a calm, contrasting surface.
  • Use the vector mark for small sizes, print, and anything that needs a crisp edge.
